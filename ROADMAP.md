# Roadmap

- Ensemble Kalman filter option with confidence bands on speeds and travel times
- Origin–destination demand estimated from toll entry/exit transactions
- Vehicle classes (cars, buses, trucks, motorcycles) with class-specific plaza service times
- Both directions and connected street links (network CTM / link transmission model)
- Travel-time forecasts for variable message signs, with error tracking
- Replay of recorded days for calibration and regression testing against field data
- DATEX II import/export for detector data and situation records
